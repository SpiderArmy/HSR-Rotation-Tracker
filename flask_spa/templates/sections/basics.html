Plan your rotation

Set each unit's speed, see who acts when, then choose each action while watching your skill points.

Action Value (AV)
Time until a unit acts: 10000 ÷ SPD. The lowest AV goes first, so a higher speed means more turns.
Cycles
Cycle 0 lasts 150 AV. Every cycle after that lasts 100 AV.
Skill points (SP)
The team starts with 3 and holds at most 5. A Basic attack gives 1, a Skill costs 1, and an Ultimate is free.

Character presets are editable examples, and base speeds can change between patches. Check them in game. This is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with HoYoverse.
